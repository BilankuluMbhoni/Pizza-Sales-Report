# Pizza Sales Analysis README

## Project Overview
This project analyzes sales data from a pizza business spanning January to December 2015. The goal was to uncover trends, strengths, and opportunities to drive growth. The analysis involved **48,621 rows of data** processed using **SQL Server** and visualized through **Power BI**, with both tools yielding consistent results.

---

## Data Sources & Tools
- **Data Source**: Transactional sales data (48,621 rows).  
- **Tools**: SQL Server (data extraction, cleaning, aggregation) and Power BI (visualization, dashboard creation).  
- **Timeframe**: January 15, 2015 – December 15, 2015.  

---

## Key Insights

### 1. Overall Performance
- **Total Revenue**: R817,860  
- **Total Orders**: 21,350  
- **Pizzas Sold**: 49,574  
- **Avg Order Value**: R38.31  
- **Avg Pizzas per Order**: 2.32  

**Takeaway**: Opportunity to upsell (e.g., combos, sides) to increase revenue per transaction.

---

### 2. Busiest Days
- **Peak Day**: Friday (3,538 orders) due to weekend demand.  
- **Slowest Day**: Sunday (2,624 orders).  

**Action**: Allocate more staff on Fridays and test Sunday promotions (e.g., "Family Deal") to boost sales.

---

### 3. Monthly Trends
- **Peak Month**: July (1,935 orders), likely due to holidays.  
- **Lowest Months**: September (1,661) and October (1,646).  

**Quick Win**: Launch a "Back-to-School" promotion in September to counter slumps.

---

### 4. Pizza Size Preferences
- **Large Pizzas** drove 46% of revenue (R375,319).  
- **XX-Large** contributed minimally (R1,007).  

**Recommendation**: Phase out XX-Large or promote Large pizzas as the best value.

---

### 5. Category Performance
- **Classic Pizzas**: Top seller (14,888 units), indicating strong customer loyalty.  
- **Supreme Pizzas**: High revenue despite lower sales, suggesting premium pricing.  

**Opportunity**: Bundle Supreme pizzas with sides/drinks to increase volume without discounts.

---

### 6. Flavor Popularity
- **Top Flavors**: Classic (15K), Supreme (12K), Veggie (12K), Chicken (11K).  
**Action**: Create new combos (e.g., "Classic + Supreme") and push limited-time offers.

---

## Recommendations
1. **Upselling**: Introduce combo deals (e.g., pizza + drink/side for R20).  
2. **Promotions**: Target Sundays and low-sales months (September/October) with themed discounts.  
3. **Menu Optimization**: Focus on Large pizzas and consider phasing out XX-Large.  
4. **Bundling**: Pair premium Supreme pizzas with sides to boost volume.
